Prompting Type	One-Line Explanation
1. Zero-Shot Prompting		: No examples provided → AI generates a response.
2. One-Shot Prompting		: Exactly one example provided → AI follows the example.
3. Few-Shot Prompting		: Multiple examples provided → AI follows the pattern.
4. Role-Based Prompting		: Assign a specific role to AI → AI responds according to that role.
5. Step-by-Step Prompting	: Provide or request ordered steps → AI explains or performs the task step by step.
6. Chain-of-Thought (CoT) 	: Encourage logical reasoning through intermediate steps → AI works toward a solution.
7. Contextual Prompting		: Provide background information or context → AI generates a relevant response.
8. Instruction-Based Prompting	: Give clear instructions → AI follows the specified task requirements.
9. Output-Based (Output-Format) : Specify the output format → AI responds as a table, JSON, list, etc.
10. Negative Prompting		: Specify what AI should avoid or exclude → AI generates a response without those elements.
