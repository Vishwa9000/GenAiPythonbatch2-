A token does not have a fixed size.
	It can contain one character, part of a word, an entire word, or even a sequence of words.

Key point: Token size is not fixed at 4 characters or 1 word. 
	The often-used approximation of 1 token ≈ 4 English characters is an average, not a tokenization rule.
